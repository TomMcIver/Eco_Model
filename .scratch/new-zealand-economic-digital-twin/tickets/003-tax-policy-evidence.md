# Research NZ under-25 income-tax rules and aggregate costing evidence

Status: closed
Type: research
Blocked by: none

## Question

What statutory and public-data evidence is required to calculate a proposed NZ employment-income tax exemption for people under 25? Verify current and scheduled rules from authoritative NZ sources, including income definitions, tax brackets, ACC levies, self-employment, student-loan repayments, and interactions with transfers/benefits. Identify publicly available age-by-income and employment distributions, tax aggregates, and their population coverage, years, units, caveats, and access restrictions. Assess whether an aggregate static revenue estimate is supportable without IDI or invented microdata; if so, specify a defensible estimation and uncertainty approach. Separate tax-law mechanics from behavioural or macroeconomic effects.

## Deliverable

Write source-cited findings to `docs/research/nz-under25-tax-evidence.md`. Do not provide an unsupported policy cost or treat a synthetic example as empirical evidence.

## Resolution

Research is recorded in [`docs/research/nz-under25-tax-evidence.md`](../../../docs/research/nz-under25-tax-evidence.md). Current public data provide useful age-level income/employment summaries and age- or income-band tax aggregates, but I did not verify a sufficient recurring joint cross-tabulation to estimate this specific employment-income exemption's aggregate cost. Recommend no aggregate point estimate without such a cross-tab or a transparent grouped-data uncertainty method. Individual calculations are exact once policy eligibility and interactions are parameterized.

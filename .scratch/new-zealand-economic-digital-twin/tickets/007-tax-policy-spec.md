# Specify the under-25 income-tax exemption base case

Status: closed
Type: grilling
Assignee: Codex
Blocked by: 003-tax-policy-evidence.md; 002-nz-data-catalog.md

## Question

Which explicit policy rules should define the first exact individual static calculator and the base-case scenario for the proposed under-25 employment-income tax exemption?

## Recommended answer

- Effective from 1 April 2027, with no end date in the base case.
- Eligibility is assessed when qualifying employment income is paid: the person is eligible while younger than 25; income paid on or after their 25th birthday is not exempt.
- Exempt PAYE salary and wages only in the first implementation. Keep self-employment income outside the base case and represent it as a future scenario requiring its own rules.
- Recompute annual income-tax liability under the current tax-year-specific marginal schedule after removing eligible income. Keep ACC earner levy, student-loan repayment, KiwiSaver, and non-tax payroll deductions separate and unchanged.
- Keep transfers and benefit abatements under existing rules, based on their current assessable-income definitions. Show resulting disposable-income changes only where these rules are explicitly represented.
- Use government borrowing as the first scenario's financing assumption; defer spending reductions and replacement taxes to explicit alternative scenarios.

These are policy choices, not claims about the effects or fiscal cost of an enacted proposal. Aggregate cost remains subject to adequate public data and uncertainty analysis.

## Resolution

For the first static scenario, use a 1 April 2027 start with no scheduled end date. Eligibility is assessed when PAYE salary or wages are paid; payment on or after the 25th birthday is not exempt. Recompute annual income tax after excluding qualifying PAYE salary and wages, while keeping ACC, student-loan repayments, KiwiSaver and other payroll deductions separate and unchanged. Keep self-employment income out of the base case. Apply existing transfer and benefit income definitions only where implemented explicitly. Assume government borrowing; treat spending cuts and replacement taxes as separate scenarios. These choices define a static scenario, not behavioral or macroeconomic effects.

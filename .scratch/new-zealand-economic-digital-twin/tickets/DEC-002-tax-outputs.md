# Define first static tax-slice outputs

Status: closed
Type: grilling
Blocked by: none

## Question

Should the initial static tax calculator report individual disposable-income changes, aggregate government revenue impacts, or both—and what evidence threshold governs aggregate estimates?

## Resolution

Implement both. Individual calculations must be exact under specified tax rules. Research public NZ income distributions, employment statistics, and tax data for aggregate costing. Produce aggregate estimates with uncertainty intervals and documented assumptions only if adequate evidence exists; otherwise report the limitation. Do not assume access to restricted microdata or invent figures.

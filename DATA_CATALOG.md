# New Zealand Economic Data Catalog

**Research date:** 2026-10-28  
**Scope:** Candidate public data for the first forecasting/tax-policy slice and later state expansion. This is a verified-source inventory, not a claim that every series has been ingested or has point-in-time history.

## Findings for the first slice

- Stats NZ publishes all three agreed targets as official quarterly national series: chain-volume GDP, CPI, and the Household Labour Force Survey (HLFS) unemployment rate. The latest releases provide downloadable CSV/XLSX and link to time-series tables. GDP is generally released about 11.5 weeks after quarter-end; the CPI and HLFS release pages provide their release calendars. Stats NZ is replacing Infoshare with Aotearoa Data Explorer (ADE); the ADE API requires an API Portal account/subscription, and Infoshare remains available while migration proceeds. [GDP series and revision policy](https://www.stats.govt.nz/corporate/revision-policy-macro-economic-statistics/), [GDP indicator](https://www.stats.govt.nz/indicators/gross-domestic-product-gdp/), [CPI release](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/consumers-price-index-september-2026-quarter/), [HLFS release](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/labour-market-statistics-june-2026-quarter/), [Infoshare](https://www.stats.govt.nz/tools/stats-infoshare/), [ADE API guide](https://portal.apis.stats.govt.nz/api-guide), [API subscription](https://portal.apis.stats.govt.nz/how-to-subscribe).
- Revisions are material for backtesting. Stats NZ revises GDP at ordinary releases and incorporates new annual benchmarks in the December release; its published macro revision policy says historical revisions commonly reach at least two years and can extend further for new information or methodology changes. It also publishes GDP revision-pattern tables. HLFS seasonal estimates can change as new observations arrive, and release corrections occur. Save each downloadable release and its metadata; do not treat a current download as the value known at an old forecast date. [Stats NZ macro revision policy](https://www.stats.govt.nz/corporate/revision-policy-macro-economic-statistics/), [GDP revisions page](https://www.stats.govt.nz/publications/?filters=National+accounts&topicFiltersID=1055), [HLFS period-specific quality notes](https://datainfoplus.stats.govt.nz/item/example.org/19f13c9d-878c-4055-88c1-5ad70a1169c0/12).
- A public-data route exists for age-specific income evidence, but the public tables do not automatically supply the joint distribution needed for an exact aggregate employment-income tax cost. Inland Revenue (IRD) publishes annual wage/salary earner counts and wage totals by income band, but the described tables are not age-cross-tabulated. IRD separately publishes taxable-income and PIE measures by age/income; taxable income includes more than employment wages. Stats NZ's HLFS income release reports wages/salaries, self-employment, transfers, and other income by age and other groups, but survey estimates have sampling error and public outputs are summary statistics rather than person-level microdata. A defensible aggregate exemption estimate therefore needs a documented method combining compatible public aggregates, with uncertainty and sensitivity analysis; first verify cell definitions and overlap. If this cannot bound the needed joint distribution, report the estimate as not identified. [IRD wage/salary distributions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/about-us/tax-statistics/revenue-refunds/wage-salary-distributions), [IRD taxable-income distributions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/about-us/tax-statistics/revenue-refunds/income-distribution), [Stats NZ HLFS income release](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/labour-market-statistics-income-june-2026-quarter/), [HLFS income DataInfo+](https://datainfoplus.stats.govt.nz/item/example.org/8d1f8525-746e-43cd-ad82-81a96f46225f).
- Public release archives are not equivalent to a maintained real-time vintage database. GDP revision-pattern data and historical releases are promising. A complete machine-readable vintage series for CPI, HLFS, and every feature was not verified in this research. The pipeline should archive raw files and release timestamps itself as of ingestion, and the backtest should fail closed when a required historical vintage is absent.

## Restricted and non-public data

- **Stats NZ Integrated Data Infrastructure (IDI): restricted microdata, not a public-download source.** Access requires an approved statistical/public-good project and approved researchers, and data are accessed through secure Data Lab/approved remote arrangements with confidentiality checks. The linked IRD employee-monthly schedule, individual tax records, and detailed household-level cross-agency joins described in Stats NZ metadata must not be treated as accessible to this repository. Public aggregate releases derived from administrative data are a distinct product and can be used subject to their own disclosure/licence rules. [Stats NZ IDI privacy assessment](https://www.stats.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/idi-overarching-pia.pdf), [HILS metadata describing integrated inputs](https://datainfoplus.stats.govt.nz/item/nz.govt.stats/1ed1e923-4c10-407c-95a0-192be61afcf2).
- **IRD person-level returns, PAYE and linked records:** access for IRD operations or controlled Stats NZ statistical research is not equivalent to public access. Public IRD wage/tax tables are aggregates and do not provide person records. [IRD open-data/privacy statement](https://www.ird.govt.nz/about-us/open-data/inland-revenue), [Stats NZ IDI privacy assessment](https://www.stats.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/idi-overarching-pia.pdf).
- **RBNZ regulatory returns and other agency administrative records:** public statistical tables may aggregate them; source microdata are not implied to be open by their publication.

## Licensing, vintage, and ingestion rules

1. Keep original downloaded files unchanged and record SHA-256, URL, retrieval timestamp (UTC), release/publication timestamp, reference period, source series/table ID, licence/attribution, file format, and any response headers/ETag. For mutable pages, retain the release page and metadata alongside the file.
2. Distinguish **reference period** from **publication time** and **database extraction/retrieval time**. A value belongs in a simulated information set only if its publication timestamp is at or before that forecast origin.
3. Treat every agency's current downloadable workbook as a mutable latest vintage. IRD explicitly says tax statistics are dynamic and current at extraction date; its current annual tables can revise past years. Stats NZ release pages document revisions/corrections. Version the bytes, not only the parsed values.
4. Obtain the precise Stats NZ ADE API terms/API key and verify endpoint availability during implementation. Stats NZ says API Portal content is CC BY 4.0, but API subscriptions require registration and a key. Infoshare is being replaced; do not make a fragile undocumented scrape the only ingestion path. RBNZ asks automated scrapers to follow its terms.
5. Check each dataset's metadata and source-specific licence before redistribution. The general Stats NZ/IRD terms above do not automatically govern third-party data displayed on those sites. OECD, IMF, World Bank, and ILO have dataset-level exceptions or attribution conditions.

## Priority and unresolved checks

1. **First ingest candidates:** Stats NZ quarterly GDP, CPI, and HLFS unemployment; obtain their source identifiers, release files, and available revision/vintage records. Keep seasonal adjustment and units explicit.
2. **Tax-costing feasibility:** acquire and inspect the public IRD workbook schema and Stats NZ HLFS income tables for exact age bands, wage/source definitions, income bins, suppression, and dates. The sources show useful aggregates exist, but the available public descriptions do not establish a jointly observed under-25 employment-income distribution at the tax-rule resolution. Do not publish an aggregate number until that joint-distribution issue and uncertainty method are resolved.
3. **Supplemental state:** evaluate RBNZ OCR/rates/credit, Stats NZ national accounts components, Treasury fiscal series, MSD transfers, and population data after the target-series pipeline works.
4. **Vintage acceptance gate:** inventory per-release raw snapshots and the GDP revision tables; test whether the first backtest can reconstruct the actual forecast information set for each origin. If not, shorten the backtest window or label the results revised-data pseudo-out-of-sample, never a real-time vintage backtest.
